Safety Terms

Last updated: November 20, 2026

The service

SheSafe lists venues and services that position themselves as safe and hygiene-conscious for women. Listings are provided by the merchants themselves.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information when registering or onboarding a venue.
  • Keep your account credentials private.
  • Use the platform respectfully; no fake listings, abuse, or misuse of bookings.

Vendor responsibilities

Merchants are responsible for the accuracy of their listings, the services they deliver, and honoring bookings they accept through the platform.

Limitations

SheSafe connects users and venues but is not a party to the services booked. Safety and hygiene labels are informational and do not replace your own judgment. The app is provided as-is, without warranties.
