Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What SheSafe is

SheSafe is a demonstration application that helps users discover women-safe venues and services nearby. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash) when you register.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google to create or sign in to your account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Vendor information: business name, category, location and contact details provided by merchants during onboarding.
  • Location you pick: the map location you choose when exploring or onboarding, used only to show nearby venues.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To show venues and bookings relevant to you.
  • To let vendors manage their listings and bookings.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Sign-In (optional), OpenStreetMap/OpenFreeMap map tiles, and Nominatim for address lookup. These services receive only the technical requests needed to display maps or sign you in.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. To update or delete your account information, contact us at the email below.
