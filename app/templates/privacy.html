Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

What this is

SheSafe is a demonstration application for venue discovery and booking. This page describes what the app currently does with information.

What is stored

When you register, the app stores your name, email address and a cryptographic hash of your password (never the password itself). If you sign in with Google, the app receives and stores your name and email address from Google, nothing else. When a venue is onboarded, its business name, category, contact details and chosen location (coordinates and address) are stored. Bookings you make yourself are really stored; the venue listings currently shown are demo listings.

Your location on the Explore map

If you allow location access, your browser uses it inside the page to sort venues by distance. Your coordinates are not sent to our servers and not stored by us. Note that loading the map itself sends standard web requests to the map provider (OpenFreeMap), which receives your browser's IP address and the map area you view, which may be the area around your location.

Third-party requests

Every page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Map pages load tiles from OpenFreeMap. During vendor onboarding, your browser asks OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service for the address of the picked coordinates. If you use Google sign-in, Google handles that sign-in. Some images are loaded from svgrepo.com, googleusercontent.com and unsplash.com.

Where the app runs

The main site is hosted on Vercel and its data is stored in a Postgres database on Neon. A second copy of the app also runs on Render (shesafe-1rad.onrender.com); anything you enter there is processed and stored by Render separately, in temporary demo storage.
